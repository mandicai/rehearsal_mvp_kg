Participants

Welcome to our formative study!

We are interested in how different audiences make sense of a research presentation as it is presented. For example, recall a research talk that lost you early on, which then shaped how well you understood the rest of the talk. Alternatively, successful research talks build up understanding progressively to communicate their main takeaways.

Here's what to expect over the next hour or so:

  1. A few introductory questions about research talks you have watched (~10 minutes)
  2. Watch a short research presentation, start to finish (~10 minutes)
  3. Reflect on what you watched—your main takeaways, what helped or confused you, and how those pieces connect (~30 minutes)
  4. Rate the presentation overall (~5 minutes)
